Before we begin

Good questions.
Straight
answers.

Clarity comes before commitment.

Is it really free to start?

There is no upfront agency setup fee. We agree on the partnership scope and earn a share of revenue generated together. Any external platform costs are discussed before you commit.

How does YGA make money?

YGA earns an agreed percentage of the revenue from the product we build together. The percentage, payment process and responsibilities are set out before work begins.

Do I need a large audience?

No. Our initial focus is creators with approximately 2,000–100,000 followers or subscribers. We also look at audience trust, activity, expertise and recurring demand. Follower count alone doesn’t determine fit.

What products can we create?

We create complete custom guides, ebooks, workbooks and creator-led courses. We handle the research, original content, writing, structure and design behind the scenes. The format and every detail are tailored to your content, brand and specific niche.

Is the entire product custom to me?

Yes. We create the full product from the ground up, including what goes inside it and how it looks. We research your audience and niche, use your content and expertise to guide the original writing, and design everything around your brand. You review and approve the direction; we do the product work behind the scenes.

What will I need to contribute?

We use your existing content, expertise and brand as the starting point, then create the product itself. You review accuracy, approve the direction and share the finished product with your audience. For courses with video teaching, you remain the real creator on camera; we develop the curriculum, lesson scripts and agreed production.

Can I keep creating my usual content?

Yes. You keep creating your usual content while we research, write, structure, design and build your digital product behind the scenes. We agree on the review and launch involvement needed from you.

Are results guaranteed?

No. We use research and validation to make better decisions, but sales depend on demand, audience response, the offer and the launch. We won’t promise earnings we can’t guarantee.

What happens before we commit?

We review your application, discuss fit and explore a product opportunity. Before starting, we agree on scope, revenue share, ownership, responsibilities and external costs in writing.

01↔

Start with your world.

Your expertise, ambitions and relationship with your audience set the direction. We look for a partnership that makes sense on both sides.

02◎

Find the recurring need.

Comments, questions and existing offers reveal patterns. We study both Instagram and YouTube where relevant, including what people ask after watching.

03✓

Test the idea first.

A focused promise, a useful sample and an interest page can help reveal whether an idea deserves a full product. Interest guides us; it does not guarantee sales.

04▤

Make the useful thing.

We create the complete custom product: research, original writing, structure, cover, visuals and finished design. Your content, brand and niche guide the work; you review and approve it.

05↗

Bring it to your people.

Your content, a clear product page and a considered email sequence work together. The launch should sound like you and explain what the product actually helps with.

06↻

Learn. Improve. Repeat.

Customer questions and real responses show where to improve. We review the offer and experience together, then decide on the next useful change.
